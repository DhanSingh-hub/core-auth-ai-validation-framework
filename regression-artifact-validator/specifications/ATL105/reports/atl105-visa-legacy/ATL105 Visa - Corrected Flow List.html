What changed vs. the original Flow Coverage Report

• Counted per transaction, not per message. The original report bucketed each of the 529 messages as its own “flow” (which is why Void = 134 topped its list). Grouping messages into their parent transactions yields 273 transactions across 14 distinct end-to-end flows.
• Two flows were missing entirely from the original report and are now surfaced (highlighted above): Authorization → Completion → Void (5 txn) and Authorization → Completion → Timeout (4 txn). The original only depicted Authorization → Completion → Dup Completion among 3-step flows.
• Multi-step flows were badly undercounted because most chain members were mis-filed as standalone messages. Corrected transaction counts: Authorization→Completion 61 (orig 15), Authorization→Void 54 (orig 9), Sale→Void 47 (orig 6), Sale→Timeout 32 (orig 5), Refund→Void 35 (orig 9).
• Void, Completion, Timeout and Refund are not standalone flows. Every one of those messages is the tail of a chain, so they no longer appear as 1-step flows.
• Partial Approval, AVS/CVV, AFP, eWallet (Apple/Google Pay) and Moneris are attributes, not flows. They are entry-mode / response overlays on the chains above (e.g. eWallet auths are ordinary Authorization→Completion / Authorization→Cancellation transactions) and remain tracked as tags in the original report’s sections 3–6.
