Recommended improvement plan

Build on the current foundation

01

Enrich source context

Add source page, section, quote, segment/message-family scope, and applicability conditions for the 3,332 BRs awaiting segment resolution. Route genuine ambiguity to authorized review.

02

Extend anchors for non-element rules

For the 2,728 BRs without numeric element anchors, distinguish transaction/process, response, cross-field, lifecycle, and host/transport rules. Use a domain anchor where appropriate; preserve source meaning.

03

Sharpen requirement testability

State conditions, required behavior, exceptions, and expected outcomes. Split independently testable obligations while retaining provenance; distinguish normative text from examples and model inference.

04

Confirm candidate relationships

Review the 42 exact-anchor misses and 455 ambiguous/composite candidates; reconcile 323 legacy-anchor candidates. Record source-backed rationale for each accepted mapping.

05

Sustain complete data joins

Maintain payload and matching test-case/scenario metadata as new cases are added, and verify scenarios exercise linked BRs with assertions for expected behavior.

06

Track progress consistently

Publish the 601 source-derived and 605 catalogued views side by side. Add semantic-approved and executed measures when authorized decisions and corresponding test evidence are available.
