Recommended improvement plan

Improve traceability first; earn correctness through review

01

Repair source context and anchors

Resolve segment/message-family context for 3,332 BRs. For 2,728 without numeric element anchors, use explicit types for transaction/process, response, cross-field, lifecycle, or host/transport requirements where supported by the source. Require source version, page/section, quote, and applicability condition.

02

Make each requirement independently reviewable

Write one testable normative obligation per BR. Split independently pass/fail conditions while retaining parent provenance. Separate normative rules from examples, inferred values, recommendations, and process descriptions. Keep confidence and extraction flags visible in the review queue.

The intake analysis found at least one flag on 3,849/6,880 BR records and 9,255/12,254 scenarios; those flags should drive review, not be hidden by catalog approval labels.

03

Resolve exact-anchor misses and candidate ambiguity

Review all 66 exact-anchor misses and classify each as out of scope, new requirement, incorrect anchor, or possible independent-baseline gap. Triage 431 ambiguous/composite and 323 legacy-anchor candidates at the pair level. Preserve legitimate one-to-many links with a rationale.

04

Strengthen every test chain

Link each in-scope AI BR to scenarios that name it and exercise its full condition. Require test cases to include explicit assertions and expected outcomes tied to the BR. Mark model-inferred values as inferred, not authoritative oracles. Resolve or disposition the 27 test-case records with incomplete payload or metadata.

05

Run a staged business adjudication

Business SMEs review priority segments and flagged/ambiguous pairs. Test Solution owners record MATCH, PARTIAL, NOT_MATCH, or NOT_APPLICABLE with rationale and source evidence. Test owners then validate scenarios, assertions, outcomes, and payloads. Recompute metrics from a hash-frozen catalog and report candidate, complete-chain, semantic-approved, and executed coverage separately.
