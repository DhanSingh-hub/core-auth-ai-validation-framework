# Segment 157 Companion-Segment Compatibility: SME and TBA Note

## What Is Known

- Segment 157 is mutually exclusive with Segment 102 — sending both causes a decline.
- Segment 157 requires the card to be a Comdata card — any other card type causes a decline.
- Segment 157's amounts must reconcile with Segment 100's Fuel/Nonfuel/Tax/Cash amount fields.

## What Not To Assume

- Do not assume Segment 157 can substitute for Segment 102 outside the Comdata context.
- Do not assume tax/discount/coupon product codes can appear within Segment 157 — they must not.
