Reading this index

Three different denominator conventions are used across these reports — they are not interchangeable

Package-scoped — denominator is each AI-delivered package's own rule catalog; changes with every delivery.

Independent 1,343-row catalog — denominator is a fixed, externally-authored workbook (All_TestSolution_BRs.xlsx), not part of the test framework.

Test-framework 601-rule catalog — denominator is the oracle rule catalog actually checked into the regression-artifact-validator test framework (atl105-br-approval-matrix.json); the current, recommended convention, and the only one letting two different deliveries be compared on a truly fixed, identical denominator.
